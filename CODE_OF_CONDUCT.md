# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and focused on constructive collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keep critique focused on code, design, documentation, and user outcomes.
- Respect privacy and do not share private information without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information.
- Sustained disruption of project conversations.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.
